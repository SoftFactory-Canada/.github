# SoftFactory

One pain point, one solution.

SoftFactory is a Quebec-based company that develops and markets its own software products: TektonAI and Mensor for the construction industry, and Subvention.app for identifying public funding opportunities in Canada. Each solution originates from a specific challenge observed within real-world businesses, then grows under its own brand, with its own name and dedicated website.

## How we work

- Each solution addresses a single, specific problem.
- Each solution functions independently; businesses can choose to use one or several.
- We manage every solution from end to end—from defining the problem to bringing the product to market.
- We launch a new solution whenever a genuine, identified problem warrants it.

## Submit a problem

Is there a recurring weekly task that wastes your team’s time? Email us at [info@softfactory.ca](mailto:info@softfactory.ca). Tell us what the bottleneck is, who is affected, and what it costs your organization. All information provided remains confidential, and submitting a problem is free of charge.
